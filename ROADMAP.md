# Roadmap

- **M1:** Formal state schema, invariants, and transition tests.
- **M2:** Persistent evidence graph, versioning, dependency invalidation.
- **M3:** Trusted external verification adapters and provenance records.
- **M4:** Adversarial challenge library and correlated-evidence controls.
- **M5:** Reproducible benchmark, baselines, ablations, statistical reporting.
- **M6:** Independent evaluation and domain-specific pilot.

Milestones are proposals, not delivery commitments.
