1. Purpose

This document establishes a transparent recruitment and screening procedure for independent reviewers participating in the ResearchOS external validation pilot.

The initial objective is to recruit at least two qualified reviewers for claim AI-001.

Reviewer assessments must be independent of ResearchOS predictions and must not be generated or substituted by the ResearchOS development team.

Recruitment completion does not itself constitute external validation.

3. Reviewer Eligibility

Eligible reviewers should have relevant expertise in at least one of the following:

* Neural machine translation
* Machine learning research
* Natural language processing
* AI benchmark evaluation
* Experimental reproducibility
* Scientific evidence assessment in AI

Relevant expertise may be demonstrated through research publications, documented technical work, or professional experience.

Formal academic credentials are useful but are not the sole qualification criterion.

The coordinator must document why each selected reviewer is qualified to assess the assigned claim.

4. Reviewer Exclusion Criteria

A candidate should not be assigned when:

1. They contributed directly to the ResearchOS prediction being evaluated.
2. They cannot assess the evidence independently.
3. They have already viewed ResearchOS predictions for the assigned claim.
4. They cannot provide an evidence-based rationale.
5. They have an unresolved conflict that materially compromises impartiality.
6. They cannot comply with the review procedure.

A relationship with the original research authors must be disclosed and evaluated rather than automatically ignored.

All exclusion decisions must be documented.

5. Recruitment Sources

Potential reviewers may be identified through:

* Relevant academic departments
* AI research laboratories
* Natural language processing research communities
* Machine learning reproducibility initiatives
* Authors of relevant independent benchmark studies
* Professional AI evaluation communities

Recruitment must be based on relevant expertise rather than anticipated agreement with the claim.

The coordinator must not select reviewers because they are expected to produce favorable ResearchOS evaluation results.

6. Recruitment Procedure

Stage A — Candidate Identification

Identify potential reviewers using publicly available professional information.

Record:

* Candidate identifier
* Relevant expertise
* Publicly documented qualifications
* Recruitment source
* Date identified

Do not place private contact information in the public repository.

Stage B — Eligibility Screening

Assess whether the candidate:

* Has relevant technical expertise.
* Can review the claim independently.
* Can disclose conflicts of interest.
* Can complete an evidence-based assessment.
* Can remain blinded to ResearchOS predictions.

Record the screening decision and rationale.

Stage C — Invitation

Provide eligible candidates with:

* A brief description of ResearchOS.
* The purpose of the external validation study.
* The expected review task.
* The approximate time commitment.
* Independence and confidentiality requirements.
* Any compensation or reimbursement arrangements.
* How assessment records will be stored and used.
* Whether findings may be published.

Participation must be voluntary.

Stage D — Acceptance

Before assigning a claim, obtain confirmation that the reviewer:

* Understands the review task.
* Agrees to independent assessment.
* Can comply with prediction blinding.
* Will disclose relevant conflicts.
* Understands how their assessment may be used.

Record acceptance without publishing unnecessary personal information.

Stage E — Assignment

Assign a stable reviewer identifier.

Provide the reviewer with the same versioned claim and evidence package used by other reviewers.

Do not disclose other reviewers’ initial decisions.

7. Independence Requirements

Each reviewer must submit their initial assessment separately.

Reviewers must not:

* View ResearchOS predictions before assessment.
* Coordinate their initial labels.
* Copy another reviewer’s rationale.
* Receive instructions to select a preferred label.
* Change an initial assessment solely to match ResearchOS.

The coordinator must preserve the original assessments.

8. Conflict-of-Interest Screening

Ask each candidate to disclose relevant relationships involving:

* The original research authors.
* Organizations associated with the claim.
* ResearchOS development.
* Relevant financial interests.
* Prior involvement in evaluating the same claim.
* Other circumstances affecting impartiality.

The coordinator must document whether a disclosed conflict is:

* Acceptable with disclosure.
* Manageable through additional safeguards.
* Disqualifying for the assignment.

Conflict decisions must be made before the assessment begins.

9. Reviewer Privacy

Public repository records should use identifiers such as:

* AI-REVIEWER-001
* AI-REVIEWER-002

Do not publish:

* Personal email addresses without permission.
* Private correspondence.
* Unnecessary identifying information.
* Confidential conflict disclosures.
* Unredacted assessment records containing private information.

Store recruitment correspondence and completed initial assessments in an appropriately access-controlled location.

Public reporting should use redacted records unless reviewers explicitly authorize additional disclosure.

11. Evidence Package Requirements

Before reviewers begin, prepare a consistent evidence package containing:

1. The exact claim wording and version.
2. The original publication identifier.
3. Relevant source locations.
4. Reported experimental conditions.
5. Supporting and contradictory evidence.
6. Evidence provenance information.
7. Known methodological limitations.
8. The assessment template.
9. The reviewer guidelines.

The package must not contain ResearchOS predictions or reference labels.

Any package updates during review must be versioned and disclosed to all affected reviewers.

12. Reviewer Assessment Submission

Reviewers must submit:

* Their assigned reviewer identifier.
* The claim version assessed.
* Evidence identifiers examined.
* One label: ACCEPT, REJECT, or UNCERTAIN.
* An evidence-based rationale.
* Relevant limitations.
* A conflict-of-interest declaration.
* Confirmation of prediction blinding.
* Assessment date.

The coordinator must retain each initial assessment unchanged.

13. Disagreement Resolution

If reviewers disagree:

1. Preserve both initial assessments.
2. Document the disagreement.
3. Identify relevant evidence or interpretation differences.
4. Seek additional independent expertise when necessary.
5. Record the adjudication process.
6. Establish a justified final reference label.

Unresolved evidentiary uncertainty may justify an UNCERTAIN reference label.

Adjudication must not be influenced by ResearchOS predictions.

14. Recruitment Audit Record

The coordinator should maintain an auditable record of:

* Number of candidates identified.
* Number screened.
* Number eligible.
* Number invited.
* Number accepting.
* Number declining.
* Number excluded.
* Reasons for exclusion.
* Reviewer assignments.
* Assessment completion.

Public reporting should present aggregate recruitment information without exposing private candidate details.

15. Completion Criteria

The AI-001 recruitment phase is complete when:

* [ ]	At least two qualified reviewers have accepted.
* [ ]	Relevant qualifications have been documented.
* [ ]	Conflicts of interest have been assessed.
* [ ]	Reviewer independence has been established.
* [ ]	Prediction blinding has been confirmed.
* [ ]	Both reviewers have received the versioned evidence package.
* [ ]	Assessment submission procedures are established.
* [ ]	Privacy protections are in place.

Recruitment completion must be distinguished from review completion.
